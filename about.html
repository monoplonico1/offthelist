About

We design the journeys no list could hold.

The best trips are not found by scrolling. They are built by listening closely, then following the details that make someone come alive.

Our purpose

Travel should feel less like a product and more like a story only you could live. We exist to replace the obvious with the meaningful.

I

We start with who

Before any destination, a questionnaire about how you move, what you avoid and what you would never admit you want. Every answer is studied in depth.

II

Places before the crowd

Places that are still themselves, reached through people who know their corner of the world deeply. Sometimes we will not even tell you where until you are at the gate.

III

With you, every step

From the day you book to the flight home, we follow your journey closely and are on hand, day and night, whenever plans need to change.

Our founder

Sofia Bennett

After fifteen years designing journeys for the biggest names in travel, Sofia noticed that the moments people remembered were rarely the ones in the brochure. They were the invitations, the wrong turns, the dinners nobody planned.

She founded Off The List to make those moments the starting point, supported by a small team of specialists and a trusted network on the ground.
