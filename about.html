Our purpose

Travel should feel less like a product. More like a story only you could live.

We exist to replace the obvious with the meaningful. To make room for curiosity, serendipity and the people who turn a place into something you remember.

No templates. No one-size-fits-all itineraries. No ticking off someone else’s highlights.

2021

The first brief arrived on one page. The answer became a company.

How it started

It started with one trip we couldn’t find anywhere.

A milestone birthday. A love of wild landscapes. A deep dislike of groups, lanyards and being told where to stand.

The plan that followed crossed mountains, kitchens and family homes — not because they were on a “must-see” list, but because every choice made sense for that person. Friends asked for their own. Then friends of friends did too. Off the List grew from there: slowly, personally, one well-listened-to traveller at a time.

“The destination matters. But how it makes you feel matters more.”

Meet our founder

Sofia Bennett

Founder · listener-in-chief · lifelong detour-taker

After fifteen years designing journeys for big travel names, Sofia realised the moments travellers remembered most were rarely the ones in the brochure. They were the invitations, wrong turns and conversations no algorithm could rank.

She founded Off the List to make those moments the starting point — supported by a small team of specialists and a trusted network of people who know their corner of the world deeply.

“Our job is to notice the sentence you almost throw away — because that’s often where your journey begins.”
