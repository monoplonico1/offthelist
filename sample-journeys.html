Central Asia

Eagle Hunters of Kyrgyzstan

Eight days chasing the life less ordinary across the Tian Shan.

Duration
8 days
Travellers
Solo
Flight
~5 hrs

Keystone experiences

  • Eagle Hunter apprenticeship — Two days with a berkutchi family in Bokonbaevo. Ride out at dawn, fly the golden eagle, stay in their yurt.
  • Kel-Suu by horseback — Remote turquoise lake reachable only on foot or horse. One night camping at 3,200m with a herder guide.
  • Manas Epic storytelling — Private performance of the world's longest oral poem, told by a manaschi in his home village.
  • Felt-making with nomad women — Craft a shyrdak in a mountain yurt camp. Leave with a piece you made yourself.

€3,800

per person · flights incl.

North Africa

Slow Morocco: Essaouira & the Atlas

Seven days of unhurried discovery. Designed around two very small travellers.

Duration
7 days
Travellers
2 adults, 2 kids
Flight
~3 hrs

Keystone experiences

  • Private sunrise harbour boat — Out before the medina wakes. A small fishing boat, just your family and a captain who knows every gannet roost.
  • Rampart sunset walk — The famous Essaouira bastions at golden hour, timed for when the wind drops and kids can run freely.
  • Chefchaouen blue alley morning — Private riad, early access before the crowds, a local guide who brings the right pace for a family.
  • Family cooking class — Tagine and flatbread in a home kitchen. Harira for the grown-ups. Msemen the kids can help fold.

From

€4,200

family total · flights incl.

Southeast Asia

Borneo: Deep into Sabah's Rainforest

Ten days in the oldest rainforest on earth, built around a 30th birthday worth remembering.

Duration
10 days
Travellers
Solo
Flight
~14 hrs

Keystone experiences

  • Kadazan-Dusun harvest ceremony — Invited into the Pesta Kaamatan celebration in a longhouse village. Tapai rice wine, bamboo music, dances that have not changed in 400 years.
  • Kinabalu summit at dawn — Southeast Asia's highest peak. Via Ferrata descent with the Crocker Range below in morning cloud.
  • Kinabatangan River night cruise — Pygmy elephants, proboscis monkeys, and freshwater crocodiles, with a local naturalist and no tour group.
  • Rungus longhouse stay — Two nights with an indigenous Rungus family. Learn to weave beaded jewellery. Eat what they eat.

From

€4,600

per person · flights incl.

South Caucasus

Armenia: The Undiscovered Caucasus

Nine days in the world's oldest Christian nation. Lavash, ancient monasteries, and a balloon over Ararat at dawn.

Duration
9 days
Travellers
Group of 4
Flight
~4.5 hrs

Keystone experiences

  • Lavash baking with a village family — The ancient flatbread, made in a tonir earth oven. UNESCO-listed, rarely done with outsiders. Lunch stays after.
  • Khosrov Forest State Reserve — One of the oldest protected forests on earth. Private jeep, local botanist, no other tourists.
  • Duduk in a stone church — A master musician plays the mournful Armenian reed flute inside a 5th-century monastery in the Debed Canyon. Acoustics that stay with you.
  • Hot air balloon over Mount Ararat — At first light from the Ararat plain. The sacred mountain the Armenian nation was born looking at, from above.

From

€3,000

per person · flights incl.
