01

Central Asia · Kyrgyzstan

The eagle and the horizon

Ten nights across the Tian Shan, for a traveller who wanted to feel small again.

Duration
10 nights
Travellers
Solo
Best season
June – Sept
  1. Dawn with the berkutchi. Two days with an eagle-hunting family on the shores of Issyk-Kul, sleeping in their private yurt.
  2. Kel-Suu on horseback. A turquoise lake reached only by horse, with a private camp at 3,200 metres.
  3. The night train east. A restored carriage to the edge of the Chinese border, dinner served as the steppe goes dark.
  4. A felt of your own. A shyrdak made by hand in a mountain camp, carried home in your luggage.

From US$18,400per person · private throughout

02

Arabian Peninsula · Oman

Wadis and silence

Eight nights between canyon water and empty beaches, for two people who needed to stop.

Duration
8 nights
Travellers
A couple
Best season
Oct – April
  1. Wadi Shab before anyone. A private swim through the canyon at first light, before the road wakes up.
  2. A night in the Empty Quarter. A camp set for two among the dunes, under more stars than you have seen.
  3. The turtle beach at Ras al Jinz. After the last visitors leave, with a conservationist and a red torch.
  4. A dhow along Musandam. Fjords, dolphins and a lunch cooked on board, with no one else on the water.

From US$14,900per person · private throughout

03

South Caucasus · Georgia

A table in the Caucasus

Nine nights of mountains, wine and long dinners, for a family of four who wanted to slow down together.

Duration
9 nights
Travellers
Family of four
Best season
May – Oct
  1. A supra in Kakheti. A traditional feast in a winemaker's garden, with the toasts explained to the children.
  2. Into Svaneti by road and foot. Medieval stone towers, mountain villages and a picnic above the glaciers.
  3. Qvevri wine with the family. Opening a clay vessel buried in the ground, eight thousand years of practice.
  4. Tbilisi after dark. Sulphur baths, a private rooftop and the old town once the day-trippers are gone.

From US$16,200per person · private throughout

04

The Arctic · Greenland

Light at the end of the world

Seven nights on the edge of the ice, for a traveller who wanted one sky they would never forget.

Duration
7 nights
Travellers
Two friends
Best season
Sept – March
  1. Ilulissat by private boat. The icefjord at midnight, among bergs the size of cathedrals, without another vessel in sight.
  2. A lodge with a glass roof. The aurora from your bed, and a wake-up call the moment it begins.
  3. Dog sled to a hunting camp. A day across the sea ice with a local musher, and a fish cooked over the stove.
  4. A helicopter to the ice sheet. Landing on two-kilometre-thick ice that has never seen a road.

From US$24,500per person · private throughout
